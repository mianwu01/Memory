The open questions as decisions

A recommendation he can veto

He responds better to a vetoable proposal than an open question. Each carries the stake and the position to present. Q1–Q3 are the priority three.

Q1 · gated vs. regime-free

Keep the gated formulation — it matches CD-NOD's input and makes events explicit. If he prefers regime-free: rework A2/A5 into nonstationary transitions, switch C1a to continuous sufficient-change. ~1–2 days.

Q2 · the gluing step

Attempt diagonal-hold transport as written, but ask whether the nonparametric-concepts machinery gives a cleaner bridge. If redirected: read the ICML 2025 proof and rebuild C1c on its skeleton first.

Q3 · is Ft the target

Keep it, with a Markov-blanket rephrasing offered as cosmetic. If he wants a soft information-theoretic variant: add an ε-sufficient relaxation without deleting the exact version the proof supports.

Q4 · gate vs. joint model

Preprocessing gate first — modular, matches the ICML 2024 framework as published. Joint modeling makes certificate (iii) a model-comparison statement; medium rework.

Q5 · Layer 1 alone

Treat as scaffolding unless E0's heatmaps are surprisingly rich. Either answer only changes packaging, not the work.

Q6 · sims during iteration

Ask permission for generator + ground-truth scaffolding only (no discovery runs) — respects R1 while removing dead time. If no: the repo skeleton is still written on paper below.

Q7 · policy-relativity

Defend A8 as honest and standard; offer cross-policy invariance as a stronger later target, not a definitional requirement. If he insists it belongs in the definition, scope it as its own section with its own conjecture rather than silently strengthening D1.

E0 runbook · precise enough to start coding

The first experiment, on paper

Discovery on the fully observed motif — no mixing, no VAE, CPU-only — so any failure is attributable to the memory regime itself. tigramite for PCMCI⁺; causal-learn for PC-stable and CD-NOD.

# repository skeleton
causal-memory/
  gen/motif.py          # episode generator
  gen/ground_truth.py   # unrolled adjacency + true frontier F_t(Y)

  disc/run_pc.py        # causal-learn PC-stable, window-unrolled

the sweep

Δ ∈ {1,2,5,10,20,50} · σ ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.1} · k ∈ {1,2,4} · simultaneous/staggered · shared/per-item · window W ∈ {Δ, 2Δ, too-small}. 10⁴ episodes/config, three algorithms, five seeds — ~1,500–2,000 runs, under a day of CPU wall-clock.

deliverable plots

Heatmap of read-edge F₁ over (Δ,σ) per k — the headline regardless of pass/fail; boundary vs. mid-hold bars (P1); simultaneous/staggered & shared/per-item (P3); selection FPR with gate on/off (P2); W-sensitivity (R8).
